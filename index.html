Circuit and network ideas

Built on the group's causal network mapping, symptom-specific targeting and accelerated TMS work.

A

Trial-level network meta-regression of sham-controlled depression RCTs

Does normative connectivity of each trial's stimulation site to the subgenual cingulate predict improvement in active arms, using sham arms as a negative control? Tests a disagreement in the group's own 2026 papers across about 100 trials.

OpenFits all five · no trial-level, arm-level version foundPitch
B

Network localization of TMS sites for PTSD, by symptom cluster

The same pipeline applied to PTSD trials (re-experiencing, avoidance, hyperarousal, negative mood), with convergence against lesion data and Sar-El as clinical lead.

Partly openAbout 15 PTSD efficacy reviews registered, none with network mapping · may already be underway in the Fox/Siddiqi labs
C

Symptom-specific circuits across trials

Dysphoric vs anxiosomatic improvement as a function of target connectivity, pooling individual patient data from the group's head-to-head and SNT trials.

Partly openDone in single cohorts (Siddiqi 2020; Kaster 2023) · depends on data sharing
D

Non-prefrontal ("posterior") TMS targets across disorders

Efficacy of occipital, parietal, TPJ and cerebellar targets, and whether effective posterior sites engage the same circuits as effective prefrontal sites.

Not checked in PROSPEROBuilds on Taylor, Nat Ment Health 2025

Other open ideas

Independent of the group's methods.

E

Component network meta-analysis of TMS add-ons in depression

Which add-on adds to TMS (SSRI vs SNRI, antipsychotic augmentation, psychotherapy), and are the effects additive? Pilot search estimates 70–100 RCTs; antidepressant data are dense, psychotherapy data are thin.

OpenNo published or registered cNMA in depression · needs Chinese databases
F

Sham response or blinding in neurological TMS

Tinnitus, Alzheimer's disease and MCI, multiple sclerosis and aphasia. Psychiatry, pain, stroke and Parkinson's disease are already covered.

Partly openNarrower scope
